03 · Confirm the days are complete

A day without a payment is only treated as zero after you confirm it. Confirm all days from the start of your history, including gaps. Adding or deleting a payment reopens that day.

Training needs at least 70 consecutive complete days and six included credit dates. Predictions extend up to 14 days past confirmed history. Beyond seven days, forecasts are exploratory and more uncertain.

Model results & how estimates work

Separate expense and incoming-money regression models use lagged spending, lagged credits, rolling averages and calendar features. Model selection uses chronological validation. Budget labels are transparent comparisons of estimates, not a separate learned classifier. A negative R² means the model performs worse than a test-set mean reference. No accuracy level is guaranteed.
